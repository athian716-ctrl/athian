SDG 8: Decent Work & Economic Growth

Highlighting a problem and a practical, code-driven solution

The Problem

Youth unemployment and underemployment reduce economic opportunity and create long-term social costs. Many job seekers lack clear pathways to upskill and connect with employers.

Proposed Solution

A simple, accessible portal that connects local employers to skilled youth through curated training resources and a lightweight matching interface. This static demo shows the concept, messaging, and onboarding flow using plain HTML and CSS.

Train

Curated micro-courses and skill badges to prepare candidates.

Match

Simple filters let employers find candidates by skill and location.

Place

Short internship & apprenticeship pathways lead to stable jobs.

How this demo is built

  1. Static HTML structure for accessibility and performance.
  2. Responsive CSS for mobile-first layout and readability.
  3. Optional: add a small JS layer to filter and simulate matches.
